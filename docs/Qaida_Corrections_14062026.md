## How to read this document

- **Page numbers** are the app's own page indicator (the `Page X / 87` shown in the
  reader's bottom bar). They were verified by walking all 87 pages of the live deployed app,
  so "Page 50" here is exactly the page the reader displays as *Page 50/87*.
- Every correction links the **screenshot** the reviewer marked up. The screenshots live in
  `check/qaida_corrections/imageN.png` (extracted from the Word file, same order).
- In the reviewer's screenshots: a **yellow** card = how the word looks **now** in the app;
  a **white/red‑coloured** card = the **correct** target form. Red arrows / red boxes / red
  text point at the exact letter or mark to fix.
- **Brief** = one‑line summary. **Detailed** = what is wrong and the exact change to make.

> ⚠️ **Numbering note:** the reviewer's *chapter* numbers (e.g. "idgham chapter 24",
> "chapter 28") come from a printed copy and do **not** match the app's chapter numbers
> (in the app Idgham = Chapter 21, Ghunnah‑at‑Meem = Chapter 23). The **page numbers above
> are authoritative** because they were taken from the running app.

# Section 1 — Global / Structural changes

These apply across the whole Qaida, not to a single page.

### G1. Remove the subtitle "ترکیب حروف" (Tarkeeb Huroof) from every chapter
- **Brief:** Delete the recurring Arabic subtitle *Tarkeeb Huroof* shown under each chapter title.
- **Detailed:** Almost every chapter (29 of them) carries `ترکیب حروف` as its Arabic subtitle,
  and several pages repeat it at page level. The reviewer wants it removed everywhere so each
  chapter shows only its real title (e.g. *Movements (Harkaat)*, *Idgham*, etc.).

### G2. Split the Qaida into "Basic" and "Advanced"
- **Brief:** Basic Qaida = **Chapters 1–15**; Advanced Qaida = **Chapters 16 onward**.
- **Detailed:** The reviewer wants the book divided in two. Chapters 1–15 (which end at app
  **Page 40**) form the *Basic Qaida*; everything from Chapter 16 (*Three Variable Letters*,
  Page 41) onward becomes the *Advanced Qaida*. This matches the note "pages 1 to 40 are Basic
  Qaida; after that start Advanced Qaida."

### G3. Add a new chapter: "Rules of Raa" (in the Advanced section)
- **Brief:** Create a dedicated chapter explaining the rules of pronouncing **ر (Raa)**.
- **Detailed:** A brand‑new chapter on the Rules of Raa (heavy/light Raa, Tafkheem/Tarqeeq) is
  to be added inside the new Advanced Qaida section.

### G4. Remove Hamza where flagged ("Hamza remove only")
- **Brief:** Drop the hamza (ء / the hamza seat on alif) on the specifically marked words.
- **Detailed:** A general instruction repeated throughout — wherever a word is shown with a
  hamza on/under the alif that should not be there, remove only the hamza (leave the rest of
  the word unchanged). The individual instances are listed in Section 2 (Pages 40, 57, 69, …).

### G5. Organise & format everything properly
- **Brief:** General polish pass.
- **Detailed:** After the above, tidy the chapter ordering, titles and overall formatting so the
  Basic/Advanced division reads cleanly.
